# Product

## Promise

Score an entire fastpitch softball game from one iPhone, recover from mistakes without fear, and finish with accurate player, pitching, pitch-count, box-score, and season statistics.

## User

One scorekeeper tracking one team. No account, backend, or multi-user collaboration is required for MVP.

## MVP outcomes

- Maintain roster and seasons.
- Create a game and batting lineup.
- Score both halves of a game.
- Track every pitch when desired, including total pitches, balls, and strikes by pitcher.
- Allow quick plate-appearance scoring when the scorer falls behind and reconcile pitch totals afterward.
- Track bases, outs, runs, common batting outcomes, baserunning, errors, and pitching changes.
- Undo and edit prior plays.
- Produce game line score, batting box score, pitching box score, and season aggregates.

## Product principles

1. Routine scoring must be fast and one-handed.
2. Pitch tracking may never block scoring the game.
3. Recorded events are the source of truth; game state and statistics are derived.
4. Local-first reliability beats cloud features for MVP.
5. Do not visually recreate a paper scorebook when a simpler digital interaction works better.
